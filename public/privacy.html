Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Fixdora is a market-research prototype exploring interest in hardware, tools and home-improvement products. This notice explains what information may be collected when you use this website.

Prototype notice: This website does not accept real orders or payments. Product information, prices, discounts and availability are illustrative.

1. Information we collect

If you register your interest, we may collect the email address and/or phone number you provide, your consent to be contacted, and basic submission details such as the submission time and campaign or source information.

Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information through this prototype.

2. How we use your information

We use submitted contact details to understand interest in Fixdora, assess potential demand and contact you about the prototype if you have agreed to be contacted.

3. Storage and service providers

Interest submissions are stored using Supabase, our database service provider. Information may be processed on its infrastructure according to the provider's applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Sharing and selling

We do not intend to sell your personal contact information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the prototype or disclosed where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Contact information should be retained only for as long as needed for the stated research purpose or applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction or deletion, contact us using the details below. Replace this section with your actual retention and deletion process before launching publicly.

6. Analytics and cookies

This notice should be updated if analytics, advertising pixels, cookies or other tracking technologies are enabled. Disclose the tools used, the information collected and the consent choices available to visitors before enabling them.

7. Your choices

Providing contact information is voluntary. Submit it only if you wish to participate in the research. You may contact us to withdraw your consent to future contact.
