About the Fixdora prototype

Fixdora is an early market-research concept for discovering hardware, tools and home-improvement products in one place.

This is a research prototype, not a live shopping service.

What you can explore

  • Browse illustrative product listings and categories.
  • Search and filter the sample catalogue.
  • Try the demo bag and wishlist interactions.
  • Register interest to help evaluate potential demand.

What is not live

  • Product listings, prices, discounts and availability are illustrative.
  • The demo bag does not create a real order.
  • No real checkout or payment is processed.
  • Delivery, inventory and fulfilment are not operational.

Why we are building it

We want to learn which products people need, what they expect from a convenient shopping experience, and whether there is sufficient interest to develop a real service in the future.
